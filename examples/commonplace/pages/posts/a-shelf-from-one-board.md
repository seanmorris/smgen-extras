One board, 1.8 metres of 200 by 25 pine, became a shelf and two brackets this weekend. The point was not economy. The point was that a shelf whose brackets come from the same board looks like one thing instead of three.

| Piece | Length | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shelf | 1200 mm | Front edge eased with a block plane |
| Brackets | 2 x 180 mm | Cut at 45 degrees, grain running up the wall |
| Offcut | 240 mm | Became the pencil tray |

The brackets are screwed from behind through the shelf, and the shelf is fixed to the wall through the brackets, so no fixings show from the front. It holds the reading pile and the pencil tray and nothing heavier, which is what it was for.
