The old ferry slip is only a walk at low water, and the tide table gives you the time but not the texture. Twice I have gone out at the published low and found the last fifty metres still under a hand's depth of water. The chart is for the harbour mouth. The slip is a mile up the estuary and the water there runs late.

So: the published low, plus forty minutes, and go in boots regardless.

What you get for the trouble is a view back at the town you cannot get from anywhere on land, and a stone platform worn smooth by a century of boots that no longer come. I sat for ten minutes and copied the shape of the far bank into the back of this notebook, badly.
