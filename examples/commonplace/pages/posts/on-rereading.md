I read forty books last year and could tell you about six of them. The other thirty-four went through me like weather. This year the rule is simpler: fewer books, each one twice, with at least a month between readings.

The second reading is where the copying happens. On the first pass I am being told a story and I am too polite to interrupt. On the second I know where it is going and can stop to write things down.

The short list to start, chosen because each one changed between readings:

- A field guide I thought was about birds and turned out to be about attention.
- A memoir of a printing shop that is really a book about tools.
- A long novel I first read too young, which is to say at the right age for being changed by it and the wrong age for understanding why.
