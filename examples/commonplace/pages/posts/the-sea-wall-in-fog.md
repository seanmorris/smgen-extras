Fog came in at seven and did not lift, so the sea wall walk became a different walk. Without the view it is a corridor of sound: the foghorn on the point every thirty seconds, gulls arguing on the slip, a halyard tapping on a mast I could not see.

The loop is short, forty minutes, and I know where the gaps in the railing are, which is the only reason I went. I would not recommend it to anyone who did not.

What I noticed, copied down at the kitchen table afterwards: distance measured by foghorn intervals is more reliable than distance measured by eye. I counted eleven blasts from the slip to the lighthouse steps, and eleven back.
