I write in books. I did not for years, out of a respect that was really a kind of fear, and the books from those years are mute: I cannot tell you what I thought of them because I did not leave myself any evidence.

The pencil is a soft one, 2B, sharpened often, because it erases cleanly and because it is slower than a pen. Slowness is the point. If a line is worth marking it is worth the half second it takes to mark it properly.

The one rule: never write a judgment, only a question or a connection. "Wrong" in a margin is useless to the person I will be in five years. "Compare the hinge entry, page 41 of the other notebook" is a gift to them.
