The harbour office prints the year's tide tables every December and had a decade of them in a drawer. They wanted the decade on the wall as one book, open at the current month.

The printouts were trimmed, guarded and sewn in annual sections, with a tab for each year. The cover is a sailcloth-blue buckram that will fade evenly in the window.

## What we learned

Guarding a stack of single sheets is slow and there is no shortcut. Budget a day per hundred leaves.
