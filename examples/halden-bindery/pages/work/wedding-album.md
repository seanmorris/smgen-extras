An album is a book that has to survive being loved. The pages are museum board with tissue interleaves, so prints lie flat and do not stick, and the binding is on posts so more leaves can be added when the family grows.

The cover cloth is a deep red bookcloth with the couple's initials blind-stamped, which means no foil to wear off.

## What we learned

Interleaving doubles the thickness and the cost. Say so at the start.
