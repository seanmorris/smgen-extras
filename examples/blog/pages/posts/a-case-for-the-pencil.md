Every project in the studio starts on paper, and every sheet of paper starts with a pencil. This is not nostalgia. A pencil is the fastest way to be wrong, and being wrong quickly is most of design.

## The three we use

1. **A soft 2B for layouts.** It skates, it smudges, it refuses to be precious.
2. **An HB clutch pencil for type.** Thin enough to draw a serif, hard enough that it stays where you put it.
3. **A carpenter's pencil for the press.** Flat, fat, and impossible to lose in the bed of a Vandercook.

The sharpener matters more than the pencil. We keep a brass wedge sharpener on the bench and a long-point hand crank on the shelf. The hand crank wins for anything you intend to keep.

A finished drawing gets a date in the corner and goes in the flat file. Nothing is thrown away until the job ships.
