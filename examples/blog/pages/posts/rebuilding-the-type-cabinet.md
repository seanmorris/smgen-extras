The type cabinet came to us from a closed print shop two towns over. Forty drawers, half of them labelled in a hand we could not read, and a smell of machine oil that has not left the room since.

## Sorting

We emptied every drawer onto the big table and sorted by face, then by size, then by what was actually usable. A surprising amount of it was not. Thirty years of pied type does not sort itself.

The keepers:

- A 24 point grotesque with most of its lowercase intact.
- A 36 point slab that is missing every capital Q, which we have decided is a feature.
- Three drawers of a Caslon that someone clearly loved.
- A single drawer of wood type, 8 line, that smells of something other than oil.

The rest went to a school workshop that teaches letterpress on Saturdays. Type should be used.

## The cabinet itself

New runners, one new drawer front, and a coat of oil. It stands against the north wall now, which is the only wall long enough, and the room is a better shape for it.
