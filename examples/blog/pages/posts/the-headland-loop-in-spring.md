The headland loop is the long walk, the one that takes a whole morning and most of a flask. In spring it is worth every minute of it.

## The route

Start at the coastguard cottages, follow the cliff path north until the stile with the broken top rail, then inland along the stream to the mill, and back along the top road. Eleven kilometres if you do not detour to the cove. Twelve and a half if you do, and you should.

## Seen

- Thrift in flower on the cliff edge, two weeks earlier than last year.
- A kestrel holding position above the mill field for a full minute.
- The stile, still broken. Someone has tied a bit of rope to it.

The café at the cottages opens at ten on weekends. Time the finish accordingly.
