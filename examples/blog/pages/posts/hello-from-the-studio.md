Field Notes is the journal of a small print and design studio on the harbour. There are three of us, one press, one very good kettle, and a view of the slipway that distracts everyone equally.

## What we make

Cards, broadsheets, the occasional book, and websites for people who want them to last. The press is a Vandercook from 1962. The websites are plain files. Both approaches have the same reason behind them.

## What this is for

Notes. Walks. The odd recipe for ink. We will not post often and we will try to post well. There is a feed if you prefer to be told when something new is here.

Thanks for reading. The kettle is on.
