The fog came in at seven and did not move for the rest of the morning. I walked the sea wall anyway, because the loop is short and I know where the gaps in the railing are.

What you lose in a view you gain in sound. The foghorn at the point runs every thirty seconds, and between blasts you can hear the gulls arguing on the slipway and a halyard tapping on a mast you cannot see.

## Things noticed

- The benches are exactly one fog-length apart. Stand at one and the next is a grey suggestion.
- Someone has painted the bollards again, this time a very specific green.
- The café opens at eight whatever the weather. The owner says fog is good for business.

I took no photographs. There was nothing to point the camera at, which is its own kind of lesson about looking.
