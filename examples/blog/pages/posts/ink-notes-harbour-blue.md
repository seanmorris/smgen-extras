The house blue has been mixed by eye for six years, which means it has been six slightly different blues. This month we weighed it.

## The recipe

| Part | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Process blue | 60 g |
| Reflex blue | 25 g |
| Transparent white | 12 g |
| Black | 3 g |

The black is the part everyone argues about. Without it the blue is cheerful; with it the blue looks like the harbour on a working day, which is the point.

Mix on the slab with a palette knife, not a brush. Scrape, fold, scrape. When the streaks stop showing, it is ready. Pull a proof on the stock you intend to print on, because this blue is two different colours on cream and on white.

The recipe is now taped inside the ink cupboard door. Future arguments can be shorter.
