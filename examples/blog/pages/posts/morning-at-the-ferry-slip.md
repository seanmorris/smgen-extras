The first ferry leaves at seven twenty, which means the slip is empty from six until the crew arrive. I go down most mornings in summer with a flask and a notebook and do not write much.

This morning a heron had the end of the slip and did not intend to share it. We stood a careful distance apart for twenty minutes. It caught two small fish. I caught nothing, having brought no line, and it seemed to know this.

## The light

July light at the slip is flat and silver until the sun clears the headland, and then for about ten minutes everything is gold, and then it is simply morning. The ten minutes are the reason to go.

The crew arrived at ten to seven. The heron left. I finished the flask and walked back along the top road with the town still asleep behind its curtains.
