People ask why we do not print more. The honest answer is that we could, and that it would ruin the thing we like about it.

## The arithmetic

A run of fifty takes an afternoon. A run of five hundred takes a week, and by day three nobody is looking at the sheets any more. The thing that makes a hand-printed card different from a digital one is that somebody looked at every one.

So the studio does small runs, and it does them properly, and it has stopped apologising for the price. A card that someone looked at costs more than a card that nobody did. That seems fair.

## What small means here

- Cards: fifty to one hundred and fifty.
- Broadsheets: one hundred to two hundred.
- Books: twenty-five, and only if we like you.

If you need five thousand of something there are excellent people who will do that, and we will give you their number.
