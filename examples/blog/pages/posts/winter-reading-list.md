The stove is lit by three in the afternoon now and the reading pile beside it is as tall as the coal scuttle. In order of how far down we have got:

1. A biography of a type designer who spent twenty years on one face and still was not satisfied. We understand.
2. A novel set entirely on a night ferry, which is the correct length for a night ferry.
3. A book of walks around a coast that is not ours but might as well be.
4. A thick volume on paper making that we are reading one chapter per week, which is the only sustainable rate.

## Reading rule

A book by the stove must be finished by the stove. Nothing from this pile goes upstairs. This rule has been broken twice and both times the book was lost for a month.
