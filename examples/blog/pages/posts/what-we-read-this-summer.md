The studio shelf has a rule: a book goes on it when two of us have read it. Here is what qualified this summer.

## The list

- **A field guide to the shoreline**, which is exactly what it says and which settled three arguments about seaweed.
- **A history of the postal service**, surprisingly gripping, and the reason we now call the morning delivery the second post.
- **A short novel about a lighthouse keeper**, read in one sitting on the ferry, then read again more slowly.
- **A manual on hand bookbinding** that is now covered in glue, which is the highest compliment.
- **A collection of letters between two printers**, in which nothing happens and everything matters.

We also started and abandoned a thick book about tides. It is a good book. It is simply too heavy to take anywhere.
