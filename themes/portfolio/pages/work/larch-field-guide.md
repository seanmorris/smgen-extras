Forty copies of a guide to one valley, written by the person who walks it most. Letterpress for the text, a two-colour linocut for the cover, and a sewn binding that opens flat on a rock.

The whole edition took a long weekend, most of it folding. A pamphlet is the fastest book there is and the hardest to make look effortless, because there is nowhere for a mistake to hide.

## What we learned

Grain direction decides whether a pamphlet lies flat. Every sheet was cut with the grain running head to tail, and the books have stayed open on tables ever since.
