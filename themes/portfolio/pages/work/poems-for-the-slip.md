A hundred and twenty copies, printed offset to keep the price down and sewn by hand to keep the feel up. The reading was at low tide on the slip; the books were sold from a crate.

Two sections instead of one, because forty-eight pages in a single section would not stay shut.

## What we learned

A bigger edition is mostly the same work repeated. Set up a folding jig and a sewing cradle before the first copy, not after the twentieth.
