The skipper asked for a book that would survive the wheelhouse. That meant a tight back so the pages stay flat on a shelf that is never level, a leather spine that can be wiped, and paper heavy enough that a wet hand does not go through it.

The lines are printed, not ruled by hand; at three hundred and twenty pages that was the right call. The page numbers are hand-stamped, which was not the right call, and the next one will be printed too.

## What we learned

A book that lives at sea needs its corners rounded. The first mock-up caught on the sleeve of an oilskin every time it was picked up.
