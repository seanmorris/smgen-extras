The register had been signed every Sunday since 1874 and was still being signed when it arrived, which decided the brief: a repair that keeps it in use, not a conservation box.

The spine was gone and the sewing had failed in the middle. It was resewn on new tapes, rebacked in calf matched to the original boards, and the torn leaves were repaired with Japanese tissue. The boards and the marbled paper are the ones it came with.

## What we learned

Ask what a book is for before deciding how to treat it. A register that gets written in needs a different repair from one that gets looked at.
