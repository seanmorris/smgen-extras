The hinge on the studio door has squeaked since the spring and I have been treating it the way most people do, which is to notice it every time and do nothing. Today I did something, in the cheap-to-expensive order that I wish I had been taught earlier.

1. Lift the door. If the squeak stops while the weight is off, the pin is dry or the knuckles are bearing on each other. A drop of wax on the pin fixes it for a year.
2. Check the screws. A loose top screw lets the door sag, and a sagging door loads one knuckle instead of three. Tighten before you lubricate, or you will lubricate a problem.
3. Only then, oil. And only on the pin, not the whole face of the hinge, which will collect dust and squeak again by autumn.

It was the screws. It is always the screws.
