A blog is written for readers. A notebook is written for the writer and left
where readers can find it. The difference shows in the entries: they are
shorter, they are dated, they do not pretend to be finished, and they are
worth more a year later than the day they were written.

That is the shape this theme is built for. Each entry gets a date in the
margin and a topic in the navigation, and nothing else is asked of it.
