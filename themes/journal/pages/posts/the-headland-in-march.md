The long loop, eleven kilometres, out past the lighthouse to the point and back by the inland path. I had not walked it since November and the headland had changed hands in the meantime: the gorse was out, the path had dried to a ribbon of pale dust, and the wind had moved round to the south.

The far-looking thinking happened, the kind that does not happen on the bad-weather route. A plan for the summer arrived whole somewhere between the point and the inland stile, and I wrote it on the back of my hand because the notebook was in the bag and I did not want to stop.

It is copied in properly now, two pages back from here. Whether it survives contact with June is another entry.
