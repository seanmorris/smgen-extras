1. Write the entry the day it happens. A week later it is a summary, and
   summaries are what blogs are for.
2. Let entries be short. Three sentences and a date is an entry.
3. Do not go back and polish. The notebook's value is that it was true when
   it was written.

The archive in the margin groups entries by month, so a gap shows. That is
fine; the gap is also true.
